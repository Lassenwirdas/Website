### Setup and Steering
Install the .apk on your quest and boot up.
Once you loaded into the starting position, you can acess the menu via the menu button on your left controller.
The default locomotion is the legacy locomotion, the locomotions and interactions are as follows:

#### Legacy Locomotion
Moving direction: View
Move trigger: Pull Vector

Move, by looking at the target, then extending one arm outwards, pulling the trigger and then the arm back to you.

Interact with the standard, trigger based grab interaction.

#### Vehicle Locomotion:
Moving direction: Car Front
Move trigger: Gas Button

trigger left -> gas
trigger right -> brake
x -> Downshift
a -> upshift
The steering is done via controller hight difference

Interaction method: Not implemented

#### Tank Locomotion:
Moving direction: Tank Front
Move trigger: Lever Logic
left trigger -> select projectile
right trigger -> destroy projectile
right joystick -> turret

The hull steering is done with the controllers as levers. Press the grip buttons and imagine your controllers as levers, extending and contracting youre entire arms.
