## Where I started

In October 2025 I started my Master's degree in Computer Science at TU Darmstadt, right after wrapping up my Bachelor's thesis on child-friendly algorithm workshops with LEGO®. Switching from "almost done" to "starting fresh" felt strange at first, but it also gave me the chance to pick topics I had always wanted to explore.

## The defining project: IVAR'26

The semester was shaped by the lecture *Interaction in Virtual and Augmented Reality* (IVAR'26). It is also the reason this website exists in the first place: the course required a blog to document our progress. Everything from setting up Unity to the final locomotion techniques is documented in the posts below.

## What I took with me

Beyond the technical side, the semester taught me a lot about scoping a project realistically, about when AI helps and when it hurts, and about how much fun it is to build something you can actually step into.
